# G1 — Comprehension Goldfish: Anu, Class 8, St. Joseph's (live V51.9.194, phone 390×844)

**Paraphrase.** I sign up with my school's code and get 10% off, then get a Student ID (ST-1-26).
- **Books and classes:** I download my textbooks from a library, and a whiteboard class teaches each page.
- **Parts:** each book has five parts. Part 1 is free in the trial. Each next part costs ₹1,800 for me, or I pay
  ₹6,300 at once for Parts 2–5.
- **Paying:** I pay by UPI and type the 12-digit reference; the bank confirms it, a receipt comes to Messages, and the
  next part opens in every book.
- **Locked parts:** they can't be read or used to make materials.
- **Doubts:** free for a while, then I need my own free Gemini key.

## Promises
- **1 Pay by part: Partly.** Prices and the discount are right. But the trial end differs: the classroom and Messages
  say "until 2026-11-09", My account says "Free trial until 2026-11-10 … due 2026-11-10". My account also shows
  "STILL TO PAY ₹7,200" as if it were a debt, though Parts 3–5 have no due date.
- **2 Paying opens parts: Partly.** Part 3 stayed locked in the classroom and Material Studio, which is correct. But
  the paid Part 2 is labelled "free" in the library.
- **3 Materials made: Partly.** "22 study materials are being made for you now", but the library shows "no materials
  yet" for Part 2, with no in-progress or failed state. The dummy key explains the failure; the child is told nothing.
- **4 UPI: Kept.** QR, amount, transaction ID and UPI reference box are all there. After the bank alert: "🎉
  Congratulations! Confirmed by the bank · receipt RCP-2026-00003".
- **5 Receipt: Partly.** All fields and printing are there, but the text is false (Blocker 1), and "Balance: Nil —
  fully paid" conflicts with "STILL TO PAY ₹5,400".
- **7 Library first: Kept, with one flaw.** Sign-in goes to "Download your study materials", filtered to Kerala · Std 8
  · Malayalam. Downloaded books start the class from the classroom, and materials sit above the board. The flaw: the
  "first pages" check shows four blank white pages (possibly the test PDF).
- **8 AI keys: Partly.** The notifications lead to Settings › Your AI keys, with the steps in both languages. But a
  failed doubt shows nothing and still uses up the allowance (Blocker 2).

## Findings
1. **Blocker — "All five parts of every book are open for you now".** The same claim is in the Messages text, the
   receipt ("All five parts are open for you") and My account ("All parts are open"). Only Part 2 opened.
2. **Blocker — a failed doubt counts against the allowance.** "Ask AI a doubt" opens a plain browser pop-up box, then
   shows no answer and no error. The next tap says "Today's free doubts on AI Guru's key are used".
3. **Major — paying ends the trial.** Before paying: "5 of 5 left today … trial until 2026-11-09". After paying on
   day 1: "You can still ask 1 short doubt a day". No one warns the parent.
4. **Major — the lock card contradicts itself.** "Part 3 … opens when you are enrolled", "Part 1, 2 is free", and in
   Malayalam "ഭാഗം 1, 2 സൗജന്യം".
5. **Major — two prices for the same part.** Material Studio says "Pay (₹2,000) to open this part"; My account says
   ₹1,800.
6. **Major — two due dates for Part 2:** 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-09.
7. **Major — My account says both "pay by UPI" and "go to the office".** "To pay a fee, please go to the school
   office" sits under the UPI box. It also shows "Next: Part 3 … ₹1,800, due ." with an empty date.
8. **Major — the doubt buttons don't match their messages.** "Type a doubt instead" shows "For voice and camera
   doubts, add a free Gemini key" and no text box. Camera and voice showed nothing new.
9. **Major — Malayalam is incomplete.** These are English only: "Joining St. Joseph's · 10% off", the fee table,
   Material Studio, and the long materials message.
10. **Nit —** "Pay ₹1,800 to Manoj Muthukrishnan" names a person, not the school.
11. **Nit —** the welcome card does not mention the school or the discount.
12. **Nit —** the floating counters cover the "UPI reference (UTR)" label on a phone.
13. **Nit —** `/ai-guru` opens a class at once; sign-up is a small link under Sign in.
14. **Nit —** Material Studio's "My textbooks" lists one of the two downloaded books.

**Malayalam for the parent:** headings, sign-up, the ID card and the UPI and key steps have it. Amounts, due dates,
receipt details and the payment messages do not, and some of the Malayalam repeats the wrong English (finding 4).
