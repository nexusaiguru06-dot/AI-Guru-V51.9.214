**Paraphrase.** A partner school's teacher finds "School Teacher" under Portals, signs up with the school ID, subject
and standards, and gets a TS- ID. They can sign in after the office approves. For their own subject and standards they
can:
- open the AI materials;
- edit them into "your school's version" ("The AI material stays as it is for other schools. Your students see your
  corrected version.");
- add materials to any part;
- set exams from their MCQs and see the scores, their students and a dated "My record".

Students join with the teacher or school ID.

## Findings
1. **Major — no approval notice.** Nothing tells the teacher they were approved; they must keep retrying sign-in.
2. **Major — the change record doesn't say what changed.** "Made the school's version of 'Force and pressure — MCQ' →
   '… (St. Joseph's)'" is all it says. Changed questions and answers are not recorded, so a disputed answer cannot be
   traced.
3. **Major — the exam timer restarts when the page is reopened:** 19:56, then 19:59 a minute later.
4. **Major — the office cannot see exam results**, only "exams: 1".
5. **Major — the teacher is not told that unpaid students can't see Part 2+ materials.** Trial students cannot see a
   new Part 2 material, and saving only says "Saved."
6. **Nit —** the recognised school does not fill the School field on the student form, and "School is needed" appears
   far from that field.
7. **Nit —** there are two teacher entries in Portals ("Teacher Portal", "School Teacher"), and the header still says
   "Sign in" for a signed-in teacher.
8. **Nit —** the Students tab mixes school-ID joiners with the teacher's own students under an unexplained ⭐, and has
   no scores.
9. **Nit —** exam dates and times are shown in US order (11/03/2026).
10. **Nit —** the classroom header says "4 materials" while the bar below says "3 study materials".
11. **Nit —** My account says both "Pay by UPI" and "go to the school office"; two trial end dates (11-10 vs 11-09).
