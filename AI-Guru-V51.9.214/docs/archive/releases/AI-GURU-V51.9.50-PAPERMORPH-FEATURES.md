# V51.9.50 — Five ideas from Papermorph: questions inside the animation, step strip + saved progress, offline class pack, class lint, AI sketch gap-filler

Why: comparing AI Guru with Papermorph (a Claude Code skill that turns textbooks into animated web books) showed five things worth taking. All five are additions: with questions switched off and no sketches, a class is built, drawn, timed and exported exactly as in V51.9.49.

- **Questions inside the animation** (`motion-class/interactive/questions.ts`, `components/ai-guru/motion-class/ClassQuestionOverlay.tsx`): the class pauses at the end of a step and the student answers *before* the board shows it. The question comes from the same plan as the board, so the app computes the answer; the AI writes nothing new.
  - `hcf-lcm`: after the tiles appear, **tap a tile on the board**. When some primes are not shared: "tap a prime both numbers have". When every tile is a shared prime (12 = 2·2·3, 18 = 2·3·3, where any tap would be right): "tap a tile that has no partner" (it goes only into the LCM). Co-prime numbers: "do they share a prime?". After the HCF: "what will the LCM be?" (4 options, exactly one right).
  - `balance-scale`: before the answer step, "what is x?" (value as written on the board, × the plan's unit).
  - Works inside `ai-custom` / learned composites. Asked once per unit, in its first Teach pass (option for every pass). The card sits on the half of the board without targets. Space / clicks cannot skip an open question. "Show answer" is always there. Toggle: **❓ Questions in class**.
  - Tap targets come from the DNA's own geometry (`hl-math`) through the new `boardMapping()` in `reels/motion/draw.ts`, which is the camera maths the frame drawing itself now uses (one function for both).
- **Step strip + saved progress** (`StepStrip.tsx`, `interactive/progress.ts`): one cell per step, as wide as the step, coloured Teach/Read × EN/ML, faded until seen, a dot for each question (green = right first time). Click any cell to jump (`ClassPlayer.goToStep`). Progress per **student** (the RAG classroom's `ai-guru:student-id`) and per **book · chapter · section**: time, step, steps seen, answers. **▶ Resume from step N** after a reload; **Reset progress**. Answers are stored as `StudentAttempt` records (student-intelligence agent's format), first answer only.
- **Offline class pack** (`offline-pack.ts`, button **📦 Offline class (one file)**): one self-contained `.html` with the class video (small MP4 from the same engine; bitrate chosen from the length, 0.25–0.9 Mb/s), the steps and transcript, the questions (tap targets already in video pixels), speed, step jump, and progress saved on the phone. No network. About 6–7 MB per minute for short classes; long classes stay near 25 MB. `exportMotionReelMp4` gained an optional `bitrate` (default unchanged, 6 Mb/s).
- **Class lint** (`motion-class/lint.ts`, `npm run lint:class`): broken names (registry ↔ builder cases, learned templates, plan DNAs, question steps), assets (reel fonts, Chilanka, Noto Sans Malayalam, the `/api/...` routes the class calls), the golden SCERT section and sample sketch, optional `--learned`, `--sketches`, `--plans` JSON exports. Checks run in about 0.2 s; tsx start-up adds about 1.5 s. Exits 1 on errors. In the page, every prepared class is checked for **missing audio**, **stale narration** (timing built for another recording, or captions that no longer match the plan), English in the Malayalam pass, and questions that could not be placed: **Class check** line under the player.
  - Fix found by the lint: prepared segments were cached by the *length* of the plan JSON, so an edit of the same length replayed the old captions and voice. The cache key is now a hash of the whole plan.
- **AI sketch gap-filler** (`dna/sketch-math.ts`, `dna/sketch-builders.ts`, `dna/sketch-library.ts`): new DNA `ai-sketch`, a declarative board (text, maths, line, arrow, rect, circle, polygon, dot; actions show, hide, move, focus, color, dim, morph, swap, box, tick). It is data, not code. The app checks every id, position, size, colour, order ("shown before used") and limit, and checks for text that overlaps or leaves the board **in English and in Malayalam**, then draws it with the motion verbs (same picture in both languages).
  - Only offered when no built-in DNA fits (`needsAi`); composing from built-in segments is still asked for first, and a sketch answer for a block that has a template is ignored. Never chosen by the selector.
  - A sketch is kept as a **candidate** for its block only, flagged "maths not checked — review". In **Free sketches**: ▶ Watch, ✓ Promote (becomes a learned DNA, `segments: ['ai-sketch']`, so similar blocks are planned from it), ✕ Reject. AI-composed templates of built-in DNAs are still saved straight to the library, as before.
  - Sample: why a triangle's angles add up to 180° (`samples/ai-sketch.ts`).
- Changed existing files (small, opt-in hooks): `interactive-player.ts` (question pause, `goToStep`, `question` in state), `MotionClassroom.tsx`, `reels/motion/draw.ts` (camera maths moved into one function, same numbers), `reels/motion/export.ts` (optional bitrate), `dna/types.ts` (+`'ai-sketch'`), `dna/builders.ts` (+1 case), `plan/validate.ts` (+sketch case; composites may use `ai-sketch`), `plan/ai.ts` (sketch option in the no-fit prompt, candidate saving), `package.json` (+`lint:class`).
- Tests: 34 new (questions 12, offline pack 4, sketch 10, lint 8). They cover: tap targets within 1 px of the tiles the board draws, in 3 formats; the HCF tap question never trivial and never impossible for every pair up to 120 with a shared prime; LCM and balance answers right exactly once; progress and resume; pack HTML safety; sketch validator, layout in both languages, EN = ML pictures; candidate → promote → selection; planner with a fake AI; lint on missing audio, stale timing, stale captions, Malayalam leaks, unplaced questions. Whole suite: 1049 tests, 1032 pass. The 17 that fail failed before this change, unchanged (old video/strategy modules). `tsc` errors: the same 68 as before, none in touched files.
- Seen in a browser (headless Chromium): the Motion Class page with a generated textbook PDF (voice off). The question pauses the class, tapping a tile grades it, Continue resumes, the step strip jumps, a reload offers "Resume from step 45" and keeps the answer. The app's own **Offline class** export (2.5 min class) downloads, opens from `file://`, plays and pauses at its first question. Standalone pack also checked at phone width (390 px): the card moves below the video and never covers a target.
- Not run here: real voice (TTS needs the network), a real chapter with a Gemini key (so no real AI-drawn sketch yet), the pack on a real Android phone. Malayalam prompts are drafts for a teacher to review. Questions exist for `hcf-lcm` and `balance-scale` only so far. On a phone held upright the HCF tiles are about 22 px; landscape gives about 48 px.
