# V51.9.51 — The common mistake, formulas read term by term, and narration wording checks

Why: three teaching rules from the 3brown1blue project (MIT licence; ideas re-implemented for this engine, no code copied) that AI Guru lacked: show the wrong idea and break it, read a new formula one term at a time, and never tell a student something is "obvious".

- **The common mistake** (`LessonArc.mistake`, `buildMistake` in `dna/arc-builders.ts`): a scene under a **COMMON MISTAKE / പതിവ് തെറ്റ്** label. "You might think…" with the wrong line typeset in red; the reason, as the line is struck out with a drawn cross and fades; then the right line in green with a tick. The planner may write one (`wrong.tex`, `why`, `right.tex`, `at: before | after`; the validator checks both formulas, the wording, both languages, and that wrong ≠ right).
  - **The app's own mistakes**, with numbers it computes (`autoMistake` in `dna/arc-math.ts`). HCF/LCM: "LCM = a × b" (too big: the shared primes are counted twice) → "LCM = a × b ÷ HCF", shown after the picture so it never spoils the in-class LCM question; none for co-prime numbers, where a × b is the LCM. Balance scale: moving a term across without changing its sign → with the sign changed, shown before the picture.
  - Switch **⚠ Common mistakes** in Motion Class (on by default). In code, `compileClass(…, { mistakes })` defaults to off, so other callers are unchanged. A plan's own mistake always wins over the app's.
- **Formula read term by term** (`formal.parts`, new `reels/motion/decompose.ts`): the RULE formula is written, dimmed, then each part (1–5, e.g. `\frac{1}{2}`, `a + b`, `h`) lights up in its own colour with a pen-drawn brace and a label saying what it MEANS ("the two parallel sides"), one caption step each; finally everything returns, colour-coded, and is boxed. Parts are found by their symbols inside the formula (a part that is not there is rejected). Labels that would collide move to the next row. A rule without parts is drawn exactly as before.
- **Narration wording** (`plan/wording.ts`): spoken lines may not say "obviously / clearly / trivially / it follows that / easy to see / of course / simply" or വ്യക്തമായും / സ്പഷ്ടമായും / നിസ്സാരമായി / തീർച്ചയായും…, and no spoken sentence may run over 25 words (English) or 40 (Malayalam). It covers captions (the quality gate sends them back to the AI), lesson-arc lines (validator) and the class lint (`wording` warnings). Textbook text read aloud is not checked. Every golden sample passes unchanged: their spoken sentences are at most about 17 words.
- Planner prompt: describes `mistake`, `formal.parts` and the wording rules.
- Tests: 14 new. The HCF mistake is checked for all 2,000+ pairs up to 120 that share a prime (wrong value really wrong, right line true). The balance mistake is checked for every a, b, x (right line gives a·x, wrong does not). Also covered: English = Malayalam pictures, red/green roles, no text overlap at any step, placement before/after, default off / option on, the plan's own mistake wins, in-class questions still placed, part finding (including the same symbol twice), dimming per step, label rows, unchanged rule scene without parts, wording in captions, arc and lint, all golden samples clean. Whole suite: 1063 tests, 1046 pass. The same 17 old failures as before; `tsc` the same 68 old errors.
- Seen by eye: the HCF mistake (EN), the balance mistake (ML), the trapezium area formula read part by part (EN and ML). This found the COMMON MISTAKE pill text overflowing its box and the rule's box covering the braces; both are fixed.
